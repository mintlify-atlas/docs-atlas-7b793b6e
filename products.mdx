## Adding a product to your order

Each product is displayed as a card on the store page. To express interest in a product, click the **Agregar** button located at the bottom right of the card.

<Note>
  The **Agregar** button is a UI element only. The store does not currently have a backend shopping cart or checkout system. Clicking the button does not add items to a cart or initiate a purchase. To place an order, use the [contact form](/contact) to reach the AuraStyle team directly.
</Note>

## Card hover animation

Product cards include a subtle lift animation on hover. When you move your cursor over a card, it translates upward by 5 pixels (`translateY(-5px)`) over a 0.3-second ease transition, providing visual feedback that the card is interactive.
