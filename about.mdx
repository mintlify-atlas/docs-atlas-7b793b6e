## Brand history

AuraStyle launched in 2025 with the mission of merging aura with fashion. From the very beginning, the team used **3ds Max** for 3D modeling — designing garments that do not simply cover, but project a unique presence.

The brand's creative process is guided by the **COBA philosophy**: Conexión, Originalidad, Belleza y Aura (Connection, Originality, Beauty, and Aura). Each piece in the collection is designed to strike a deliberate balance in what the brand calls the **aura/price ratio** — delivering meaningful style at an accessible cost.

The brand's ongoing pursuit is to show that great fashion is a constant search: new silhouettes, new materials, and new ways for aura to express itself.
