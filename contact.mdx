Use the contact form to send a message to the AuraStyle team, or find the store's address, hours, and social channels below.

<Note>
  The contact form uses a `mailto:` action. When you submit the form, your browser opens your default email client with the form data pre-filled in the message body. The email is addressed to **fran.reyes3223@gmail.com**. You must send the email from your email client for the message to be delivered.
</Note>

## Contact form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your first name(s)">
    Fill in the **Nombre completo** field with your first name(s). This field is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your last name">
    Fill in the **Apellido** field with your surname. This field is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email address">
    Fill in the **Correo electrónico** field with a valid email address. This field is required and accepts standard email format.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your WhatsApp number (optional)">
    Fill in the **WhatsApp** field with your phone number including country code (e.g., +54 9 341 000-0000). This field is optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your date of birth (optional)">
    Use the **Fecha de nacimiento** date picker to select your birth date. This field is optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your gender (optional)">
    Choose from the **Género** dropdown: **Femenino**, **Masculino**, **Otro**, or **AURA**. This field is optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add comments (optional)">
    Use the **Comentarios** textarea to write any questions, feedback, or inquiries. This field is optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach your CV (optional)">
    Click the **Tirar CV (PDF)** file picker and select a PDF file from your device. See the warning below before submitting.

    <Warning>
      The PDF file you select in the browser **cannot be transmitted through the `mailto:` form**. After the form opens your email client, you must **manually attach the PDF** to the email before sending it. The file picker is a reminder to include your CV — the attachment does not carry over automatically.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the form">
    Click **Enviar Mensaje**. Your default email client will open with the form data included in the message body, addressed to fran.reyes3223@gmail.com. Review the email and click **Send** in your email client to deliver your message.

    <Note>
      The form calls `showAlert()` on submit. This function is referenced in `javascript.js`, which is currently empty, so no alert will appear. The mailto action still works normally.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>
